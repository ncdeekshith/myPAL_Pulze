What premium means

The same 10 Reels.
A more considered finish.

The ₹65,000 tier upgrades production, rather than adding more videos.

Standard production

Clear edits, readable captions, brand styling and music treatment using agreed footage.

Premium production

More detailed concepts and scripts, planned visual sequences, refined pacing, colour correction, sound treatment and tailored motion graphics where the concept benefits.

Shoot days, crew, locations, equipment, talent and footage sourcing are scoped before kickoff. “Premium” does not imply an unlimited shoot or guaranteed reach.

Good to know

The package,
made clear.

How many posts are included?

Launch and Signature each include 10 Reels + 10 flyers. The recommended Momentum mix is 12 + 12 across two months; Growth is 15 + 15 across three months. Every package includes one website build.

Is this a monthly subscription?

Each displayed price covers the full package. Momentum runs for two months and Growth for three months. There is no automatic renewal stated here; payment milestones are agreed before kickoff.

What does the website include?

A website is part of the package. Pages, features, domain and hosting arrangements are agreed before kickoff.

What about filming, posting and ad spend?

Filming needs, asset sourcing and publishing responsibilities are confirmed with the team. Advertising spend and ongoing campaign management are not stated as included.

When will everything be delivered?

The schedule and review rounds are agreed after the brief, website scope and available assets are reviewed.
